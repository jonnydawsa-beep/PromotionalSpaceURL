Gaming deals UK

Gaming listings often bundle similar names with very different hardware. Exact configuration is essential before a price difference means anything.

What we track

  • Gaming laptops and desktop PCs
  • Gaming monitors and displays
  • Consoles and accessories
  • Selected PC components and peripherals

What has to match

  • GPU, CPU, memory, storage and display configuration
  • Exact model / MPN and bundle contents
  • New versus refurbished condition
  • Current exact-product prices across available retailers

What we avoid

  • Comparing different GPU or storage configurations
  • Treating a bundle and standalone product as equivalent
  • Publishing an affiliate offer that loses to a verified exact competitor

No filler deals

A category can legitimately have no live offers. We would rather show an empty category than publish weak matches or unverified prices.
