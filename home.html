Home deals UK

Home products can be deceptively hard to compare because dimensions, finishes, pack quantities and model families often share very similar names.

What we track

  • Furniture and home-office furniture
  • Lighting and household accessories
  • Storage and organisation products
  • Practical household equipment

What has to match

  • Model, size, finish and pack quantity where relevant
  • Exact identifiers where supplied
  • Item-price versus delivered-price basis kept explicit
  • Competing exact-product evidence before publication

What we avoid

  • Comparing different sizes or finishes as identical
  • Using an RRP we have not independently observed
  • Hiding delivery differences when reliable delivery data is available

No filler deals

A category can legitimately have no live offers. We would rather show an empty category than publish weak matches or unverified prices.
