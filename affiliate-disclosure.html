Affiliate disclosure

How affiliate links work on this site.

Advertising: a retailer button marked “Ad” or otherwise identified as advertising may be an affiliate link. If you make an eligible purchase after using it, we may receive commission.

Deal Burrow is affiliate supported. Affiliate commission can help fund the comparison service and generally does not add an extra charge to the price shown by the retailer.

Affiliate status does not prove a deal is best

Commission arrangements are not used as proof that a retailer has the best price. Saving claims should be based on observed comparison evidence and are limited to the sources available to the service.

The retailer completes the transaction

We do not take your card payment or fulfil your order. Prices, stock, delivery, returns, warranties and customer service are controlled by the retailer you visit.

Prices can change

Retailer feeds and public pages can update at different times. Always confirm the final price and terms on the retailer's website before completing a purchase.
