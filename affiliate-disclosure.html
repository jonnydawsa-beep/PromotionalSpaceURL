Affiliate disclosure

How affiliate links work on this site.

Advertising: a retailer button identified as an affiliate link may generate commission for Deal Burrow if you make an eligible purchase after using it.

Deal Burrow is affiliate supported. Affiliate commission can help fund the price-tracking and comparison service and generally does not add an extra charge to the price shown by the retailer.

Commission does not decide whether a deal passes

Affiliate status is separated from price validation. A cheaper verified exact-product offer from a non-monetised retailer can block a more expensive affiliate offer.

Affiliate status does not prove a retailer is cheapest

Saving claims are based on observed evidence and are limited to the retailer data successfully available to the service. We do not describe an offer as the cheapest on the internet unless the evidence actually supports that claim.

The retailer completes the transaction

We do not take your card payment or fulfil your order. Prices, stock, delivery, returns, warranties and customer service are controlled by the retailer you visit.

Prices can change

Retailer feeds and public pages can update at different times. Always confirm the final price and terms on the retailer's website before completing a purchase.
