Advertising disclosure

How affiliate links will work.

Advertising: after launch, product links that can generate commission will be clearly identified as advertising before you use the link.

Product Price Comparison UK is being developed as an affiliate-supported comparison service. When a clearly labelled advertising link is used to visit a retailer, and an eligible purchase is completed, we can receive a commission from that retailer or its affiliate network.

Using an advertising link does not mean that we take your payment or fulfil your order. The purchase is made directly from the retailer, and the retailer is responsible for its own prices, stock, delivery, returns, warranties and customer service.

Commission arrangements will not be presented as proof that a retailer has the best price. Price comparisons are intended to be based on the observed offer data available to the service.

The service is currently in development and does not yet publish live retailer offers.
