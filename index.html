UK comparison project · In development

Compare the same product across approved retailers.

We are building a product-comparison service that matches identical products, compares observed prices and highlights meaningful price differences.

Launch status: the comparison engine is currently in development. Live retailer offers will be added only after affiliate programme approval and testing.

How it will work

  1. Match the same product using reliable identifiers such as GTIN or manufacturer part number.
  2. Compare prices from approved online retailers.
  3. Highlight unusually competitive offers.
  4. Send users to the retailer to complete the purchase.

What we are building

Exact product matching

We aim to compare like with like rather than treating similar product titles as identical.

Price comparison

Observed retailer prices will be compared so users can quickly see meaningful differences.

Retailer checkout

Purchases, payments, delivery, returns and customer service remain with the retailer.

Initial coverage

The project is intended to cover selected UK retail categories where reliable product identifiers allow fair comparison between multiple approved merchants. Categories will be added gradually as data quality and retailer coverage are verified.
