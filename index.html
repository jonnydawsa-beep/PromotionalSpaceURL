Why this is different

We compare products, not just similar-looking titles.

Retailer feeds are noisy. A dramatic price gap can be caused by a different pack size, a bad feed value or an unrelated product. Our process is designed to reject those cases.

01

Identity matching

We prioritise reliable identifiers such as GTIN/EAN and manufacturer part number before treating two listings as the same product.

02

Price sanity checks

Unrealistic or malformed feed prices are excluded from public deal claims rather than being used to manufacture a large percentage saving.

03

Retailer checkout

We do not sell the product. You visit the retailer to confirm stock, final price, delivery, payment and returns.
