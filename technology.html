Technology

Technology deals UK

Technology is one of the easiest places for a misleading comparison: two products can have nearly identical names while differing in processor, memory, storage, panel, GPU or region-specific model code.

What we track

  • Laptops, desktops and workstations
  • Monitors and display hardware
  • Networking, storage and computer peripherals
  • Office technology and accessories

What has to match

  • Exact GTIN/EAN/UPC where available
  • Brand + manufacturer part number as a second exact identity route
  • Configuration-sensitive specs such as RAM, storage, GPU and screen size
  • Current exact-product prices before publication

What we avoid

  • Treating a lower-specification variant as the same product
  • Comparing refurbished or open-box stock with new stock as equivalent
  • Publishing a historical drop when a verified exact retailer is already cheaper

No filler deals

A category can legitimately have no live offers. We would rather show an empty category than fill it with stale prices, loosely matched products or a retailer that is already beaten by a verified exact-product competitor.

How publication works

Every public offer still has to pass the same exact-product, price-history, retailer-page and cross-retailer checks described in our methodology.
