DIY and tool deals

DIY deals need more than a brand and product family. Bare tools, kits, batteries, chargers, case bundles and generation numbers can completely change the value of an offer.

What we track

  • Power tools and cordless platforms
  • Hand tools and workshop equipment
  • Tool accessories and storage
  • Model-specific DIY equipment

What has to match

  • Exact model or manufacturer part number
  • Bare-tool versus kit contents
  • Battery voltage, capacity and included accessories
  • Current exact-retailer comparisons where available

What we avoid

  • Matching a bare tool to a battery-and-charger kit
  • Treating different generations as the same product
  • Publishing a high percentage drop when a current exact competitor is cheaper

No filler deals

A category can legitimately have no live offers. We would rather show an empty category than publish weak matches or unverified prices.

Validated DIY deals appear on the main deals page as soon as they pass the publication gate.
