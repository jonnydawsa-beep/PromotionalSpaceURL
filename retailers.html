Retailer coverage

Retailer data has two separate jobs.

Some retailers can supply monetised affiliate links. Others may only contribute comparison evidence. We keep those roles separate so commission cannot turn a worse price into a deal.

Important: a retailer can invalidate one of our deals even if we do not earn commission from that retailer. Public offers still have to pass the publication gate.

Comparison coverage is broader than monetisation

The comparison engine can use exact-product evidence from additional retailers where accessible. A cheaper verified exact match can block a more expensive affiliate offer even when that cheaper retailer is not monetised by Deal Burrow.

Why we do not publish a giant retailer logo wall

Being visible to the comparison engine is not the same as being a joined affiliate partner, and feed access can change. We would rather describe the role of retailer data accurately than imply commercial relationships that do not exist.
