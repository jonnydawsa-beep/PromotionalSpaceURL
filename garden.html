Garden

Garden deals and price tracking

Garden prices are seasonal, but seasonality does not make every sale a bargain. We keep the same exact-product and current-price rules throughout the year.

What we track

  • Garden power tools and equipment
  • Outdoor storage and maintenance products
  • Garden furniture and accessories
  • Watering and seasonal outdoor equipment

What has to match

  • Exact model and size where products have multiple variants
  • Kit contents for battery-powered equipment
  • Observed historical price rather than a promotional strike-through alone
  • Current exact competing offers before publication

What we avoid

  • Calling seasonal clearance automatically good value
  • Comparing different capacities or sizes
  • Using commission as a reason to overlook a cheaper retailer

No filler deals

A category can legitimately have no live offers. We would rather show an empty category than publish weak matches or unverified prices.

Looking for current offers?

Validated garden deals appear on the main deals page as soon as they pass the publication gate.
