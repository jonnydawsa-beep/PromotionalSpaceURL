About

A focused UK product-comparison project.

Product Price Comparison UK is an independent project currently in development. Its purpose is to compare identical products across approved online retailers and help users identify competitive offers without having to check each retailer manually.

How comparisons will be made

We are building the service around reliable product identifiers, including GTINs and manufacturer part numbers where available. The aim is to reduce false comparisons between products that merely have similar names.

What happens when a user clicks an offer

Users will be sent to the retailer's own website. The retailer remains responsible for the sale, payment, delivery, returns, warranties and customer service.

Current status

The service is in pre-launch development. Retailer coverage and public product listings will be introduced only after the relevant affiliate programmes have approved participation and the comparison data has been tested.
