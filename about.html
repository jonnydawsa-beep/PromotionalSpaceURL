About

A price tracker built to dig out worthwhile deals.

Deal Burrow monitors retailer feeds and price history, then surfaces worthwhile price drops only when the product, price and retailer evidence is strong enough to publish.

What we are trying to solve

Checking multiple retailers manually is slow, and simple title matching can produce misleading comparisons. Our approach is to automate the repetitive work while remaining conservative about what gets shown publicly.

What happens after you click

You are sent to the retailer's own website. The retailer is responsible for the sale, payment, stock, delivery, returns, warranties and customer service.

How the site is funded

Some retailer links are affiliate links. If an eligible purchase is made after using one of those links, we may receive commission. Commission does not change the price you pay and is not presented as proof that a retailer has the best price.
