About

An independent UK price-tracking publisher.

Deal Burrow monitors retailer product data, builds price history and checks exact competing offers before surfacing deals to UK shoppers.

What we cover

Our permanent coverage areas are technology, home, DIY, garden, appliances and gaming. The live feed changes as prices change, but those are the product areas the service is built to research.

Who the site is for

Deal Burrow is designed for UK consumers who want to know whether a product price is genuinely interesting before clicking through to a retailer. We focus on model-level product matching rather than coupon-code volume.

For retailers and affiliate managers

Our promotion method is content-led price tracking and deal discovery. We use exact identifiers where possible, recheck offers before publication, disclose affiliate relationships and do not present commission as evidence that an advertiser has the best price.

What happens after you click

You are sent to the retailer's own website. The retailer is responsible for the sale, payment, stock, delivery, returns, warranties and customer service.

How the site is funded

Some retailer links are affiliate links. If an eligible purchase is made after using one of those links, we may receive commission. Commission does not change the price you pay and does not override the publication checks.
