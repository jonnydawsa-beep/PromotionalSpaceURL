DIY and tool deals: compare kits properly

A buying checklist based on how exact-product price comparisons work. This is not a hands-on product review or an endorsement.

Bare tool or complete kit?

A body-only tool may exclude batteries and a charger. Match the manufacturer part number and included accessories before comparing prices.

Check compatibility

Read the manufacturer information for battery platform, voltage, fittings and intended use. A shared brand name does not prove compatibility.

Allow for delivery

Heavy tools and multi-box orders may have different delivery conditions. Unknown delivery is not treated as free on Deal Burrow.
