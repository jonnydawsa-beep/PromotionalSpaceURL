Gaming deals: compare the edition and platform

A buying checklist based on how exact-product price comparisons work. This is not a hands-on product review or an endorsement.

Match the edition

Check the console or PC platform, region, edition and included content. A digital code, physical copy and bundle are different offers.

Check hardware variants

Storage, connectivity, model revision and included controllers can differ. Compare exact identifiers instead of a product family name.

Use price evidence carefully

Look at observed history and the current checked retailers. A comparison price is not necessarily an earlier selling price, and no page proves every retailer has been checked.
