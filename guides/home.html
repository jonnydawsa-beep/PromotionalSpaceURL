Home deals: check dimensions, quantities and delivery

A buying checklist based on how exact-product price comparisons work. This is not a hands-on product review or an endorsement.

Measure before comparing

Check the product dimensions and finish against the manufacturer specification. Similar pictures can hide a different size or material.

Compare the same quantity

Count the pieces in a set or pack. Compare like-for-like quantities rather than a headline price for a smaller pack.

Check access and delivery

Large-item delivery can depend on your postcode or service level. Confirm those conditions on the retailer site before ordering.
