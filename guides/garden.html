Garden deals: match the machine and accessories

A buying checklist based on how exact-product price comparisons work. This is not a hands-on product review or an endorsement.

Compare the exact model

Check power source, manufacturer model code and what comes in the box. Bundled attachments or batteries can change the value substantially.

Check your requirements

For pressure washers, compare the manufacturer specifications for pressure, flow and included hose or attachments. Do not choose using the discount percentage alone.

Check large-item costs

Confirm postcode delivery, assembly requirements and returns conditions with the retailer. Recorded item prices do not include unverified delivery costs.
