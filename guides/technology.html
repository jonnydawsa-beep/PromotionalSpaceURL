Technology deals: compare the exact model

A buying checklist based on how exact-product price comparisons work. This is not a hands-on product review or an endorsement.

Match the full specification

A laptop or monitor family name is not enough. Match the manufacturer part number, screen size, memory, storage and keyboard layout. A cheaper variant may be a different product.

Check the total cost

Compare VAT-inclusive item prices, delivery and any compulsory charges. A small item-price saving can disappear once delivery is added.

Use the observations

Open the product page to see recorded prices and the date of each observation. An unchanged history is not evidence of a price drop.

Explore tracked products
