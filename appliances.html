Appliances

Appliance deals and price tracking

Appliance model numbers matter. Capacity, dimensions, energy characteristics, colour and retailer-specific suffixes can all turn a similar-looking listing into a different product.

What we track

  • Small household electricals
  • Kitchen and cleaning appliances
  • Model-specific home appliances
  • Selected electrical accessories

What has to match

  • Manufacturer model number and exact product identifiers
  • Capacity, dimensions and key specification differences
  • Delivered-price basis where comparable delivery data is reliable
  • Current retailer-page and competing exact-product evidence

What we avoid

  • Substituting a neighbouring model in a price comparison
  • Ignoring material delivery differences when known
  • Publishing when exact identity cannot be established confidently

No filler deals

A category can legitimately have no live offers. We would rather show an empty category than publish weak matches or unverified prices.

Looking for current offers?

Validated appliance deals appear on the main deals page as soon as they pass the publication gate.
